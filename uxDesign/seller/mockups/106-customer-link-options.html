Customer link: redesign options (plan 106 mock-up)

Redesign of the seller's "Link to a saved customer?" window and its badges. Problem list: 106-customer-link-today.html (8 scenarios, 15 unclear points). Light theme only. Tablet 1138 × 640 with rail, phone 384 × 800, one ID frame. Samples: Onde Onde, Adelaide, AUD; Anin, Lenny, Larissa, sandra; 0412 345 678. Buttons stay on one line (stacked full width on the phone instead of wrapping).

Strict rule (owner, 18 Oct): nothing is drawn that the app or the owner's rulings do not have. What is drawn comes from today's app (match list, Link, New customer, number kept only on this device, CSV backup) or from the owner's asks (duplicates with date and last digits, number field in the same step, Skip explained, when it opens). Everything else is a question in a caption, marked "Q".
