130 · Cancel customer orders not confirmed within 24 hours

DellaVee, event Sun 15 Nov. A customer order that is still "Ordered" 24 hours after it was placed, with no "WhatsApp received" tick, is cancelled by the app with a reason. Seller-entered and private orders are never touched. AUD, Adelaide.

  • Checked every hour by the app; the 24 hours count from when the order was placed.
  • Never touched: orders the seller entered herself, private orders, confirmed orders, orders with "WhatsApp received" ticked, and restored orders.
  • Cancelling deletes the delivery address as today; a restored order needs the address again on WhatsApp.
