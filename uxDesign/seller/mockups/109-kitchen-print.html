Kitchen print: the cook list on paper (plan 109 mock-up)

Target = owner, 18 Oct: "a kitchen print so a chef can print for the kitchen, or can print all in the kitchen to be cooked, and including notes too if whoever wants to print it includes it." Tablet 1138 × 640 (dark, rail Home · Menu · Orders · Private · Kitchen · Pickup & delivery · Dishes · Settings · Help · About), phone 384 × 800 (dark, compact B2 Cook row), two printed A4 pages (794 × 1123 px, black on white), one Indonesian set. Samples are the owner's data (Onde Onde, menu for Wed 21 Oct, printed Sun 18 Oct 5:08 pm; Pastel (1 buah), lalampa (each), Mild Grilled wrapped rice with basil chicken; chefs Chandra and Sari; the two notes). The counts (24/18/9 and the pickup/delivery split) and "14 orders" are made up to fill the page. No prices anywhere: the Cook data has none.

Questions for the owner (one by one)

  1. Cooked count from Count mode. Should the paper show "cooked 5 of 24" next to a dish? Recommend no: paper has its own tick box; two ticks confuse.
  2. Phone buttons. At 384 px both print buttons are icon-only (labels may not wrap). Labels keeps today's printer icon; Print needs a different icon (drawn: clipboard with lines). OK, or one "Print" icon opening a small menu "Kitchen list / Labels"?
  3. Preview page or straight to the print window? Orders print opens a preview page (Back, Print, "Which orders" choice). Here the sheet is the choice and Print goes straight to the print window. Recommend straight (less to build), but a preview would match Orders.
  4. Chef signed in: may they switch to "All chefs" or another chef in the sheet? Drawn: yes (the screen's Chef filter allows it).
  5. Private orders. The Cook rows show "Menu N · Private N". Print only the total, or add "(N private)" to the split line?
  6. Totals. Add a line with the number of orders (the Cook subtitle "From 14 orders") and/or total portions per chef? Not drawn.
  7. Page breaks when it is long: start each chef on a new page (grouped by chef)? Drawn: continuous, "Page 1 of 1" in the footer as in Orders print.
  8. Remember the choice (chef, group, notes) on the device, as plan 109 stage 1 says? Drawn as remembered; the sheet always opens on the last choice except Chef for a signed-in chef.
