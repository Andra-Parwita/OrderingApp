Patterns

Every screen in the brief uses one of these patterns. Build the pattern once, then each screen is content inside it.

main action danger banner

1 · List + side panel

Orders, Past orders, Delivery list
  • The list stays visible and keeps its scroll place.
  • Panel 384px, closes with ×. One main action pinned at the bottom.
  • Phone: the panel becomes a full screen with Back.

2 · Slide-over editor

Edit dish, New order
  • Opens over the list with a dim backdrop. EN | ID fields side by side.
  • Cancel + Save pinned at the bottom.
  • Closing with unsaved changes asks first.

3a · Settings in panes

Settings, Menu settings, Pictures, Chefs, Devices, Backup
  • Sections listed on the left; one section on screen at a time, so no long scroll.
  • Fields in 2 columns on tablet; EN | ID pairs share a row.
  • Save bar appears only after a change. Phone: section list, then the section.

3b · Wizard

First-time setup, Make a menu: Dishes → When & where → Check → Publish & share
  • Each step fits one screen. Back and Next always in the same place.
  • Back never loses what was typed; done steps can be tapped to jump back.
  • Later edits of the same things use 3a panes.

4 · Focus task

Pickup, Kitchen count mode
  • One order at a time in large type, read from arm's length.
  • One big action (Mark collected), then the next order comes up.
  • The queue stays visible, never hidden.

5 · Confirm dialog

Cancel order, Use this set, Unpublish, Delete dish, Restore backup
  • A warning, never a block: the seller can always go ahead.
  • Says who is affected: "3 people ordered Rendang. Their orders keep it."
  • Buttons say the outcome ("Keep order" / "Cancel order"), never Yes / No.

6 · Bottom sheet (phone)

Filter, More actions, Send an update, Switch person
  • Handle plus a visible Close button; rows 48px tall.
  • Never needs swiping to close.
  • On tablet the same content opens as a small menu under its button.

7 · Toast with Undo

Confirm, Mark paid, Mark collected / delivered, Send
  • Quick, reversible actions don't ask first.
  • "Wulan confirmed · Undo" for 6 seconds.
  • Announced to screen readers; never covers the main button.

8 · Hub

Pickup & delivery hub, More
  • Counts first ("6 to collect · 4 to deliver"), then big rows.
  • Each row: icon, label, one-line hint, chevron.
  • Rows a chef can't use are not shown.

9 · Compose & send

Share menu, Send an update, Paste a WhatsApp post
  • Shows the exact text that will be sent.
  • Language: ID, EN or both.
  • One button, "Share to WhatsApp"; Copy text as the backup.

10 · Empty & first run

No menu yet, No orders yet, Menu finished, empty lists
  • Says why it's empty, in plain words.
  • Always one next step; never a dead end.
  • First run is a short checklist that remembers what's done.

11 · Pages of 20

Past orders, a past menu's orders, Devices
  • Lists over 20 items split into pages of 20.
  • "21–40 of 64", Previous / Next (44px); page numbers on tablet only.
  • Search and filters apply to all pages. A live menu's orders stay one list so search finds everyone.

12 · Reuse picker

Wizard step 1 "Dishes", Saved sets, Add dish
  • Start from: Saved set · Past menu · Your dishes. Tick, mix freely.
  • Picked dishes stay listed on the right with a count; Next when ready.
  • "Save as set" is offered on every menu, and after publishing.

Rules every pattern follows

Touch · 44px targets, 8px apart; whole rows are tappable. Focus · 2px ring in the accent colour on everything. Status · always icon + word, never colour alone. Language · leave 30% extra for Indonesian; no fixed-width labels. One next step · one filled button per screen. No hidden gestures · every action has a visible button. Separation · spacing and 1px hairlines, not boxes. Banner · whole at rest; the sheet slides over it while working. Dates · the menu's cooking date, never "this week". Chefs · owner-only items are hidden, not greyed out. Warn, never block · the seller can always go ahead; warnings say who is affected. Less scrolling · split long forms into panes or steps; page long lists. Phone · native feel: no visible scrollbars, bottom bar, sheets.
