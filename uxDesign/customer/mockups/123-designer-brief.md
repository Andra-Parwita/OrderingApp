# Phone banner, new size: brief for the designer

**Size:** 1200 × 600 px, JPEG (the app makes it ~100 KB itself).
**Template:** `123-template-phone-banner-1200x600.png` (or `.svg`), same folder: draw over it, then remove the guide layer.

## Two parts, one picture
- **Bottom 1200 × 400 (y 200–600): the banner everyone sees.** Browsers, Android, tablet and iPhone. Kitchen name, logo, faces and food go here, as in today's banner.
- **Top 1200 × 200 (y 0–200): only in the iPhone app, behind the clock and battery.** Hidden everywhere else.

## The top part
- **One seamless picture, full height.** Draw the top part sharp, as one continuous scene with the bottom part (the sky, wall or decoration continues upward across the seam at y = 200). The app shows it **exactly as drawn: no blur, no fade**.
- **Background only:** sky, wall, wood, leaves, cloth, pattern. **No text, logo, faces or food**, because browsers and Android never show it.
- **iPhones show it from the seam upward:** about 145 px (iPhone 12–14) to 185 px (iPhone 15/16 Pro/Pro Max). The very top 15–55 px may be cut off.
- **iOS draws the time and battery in white on top of it:**
  - time: left, about x 80–300, y 90–150;
  - signal and battery: right, about x 900–1120, y 90–150;
  - camera pill (black): middle, about x 460–740, y 70–150.
  **Leave room for them:** keep these areas **calm and dark enough for white text** (no bright sky, white or busy detail there). Nothing is added by the app to help readability.
- **Why no text or subject in the top part:** it is hidden in browsers and on Android, and in the iPhone app it sits under the clock, camera and battery.

## Check before sending
- Cover the top 200 px with your hand: the bottom part alone must still work as the banner.
- Nothing important within 40 px of the left and right edges of the bottom part (small phones trim a little).

# Banner HP, ukuran baru: brief untuk desainer

**Ukuran:** 1200 × 600 px, JPEG (aplikasi mengecilkan sendiri ke ~100 KB).

- **Bawah 1200 × 400 (y 200–600): banner yang dilihat semua orang.** Nama dapur, logo, wajah dan makanan di sini, seperti banner sekarang.
- **Atas 1200 × 200 (y 0–200): hanya di aplikasi iPhone, di belakang jam dan baterai.** Tidak terlihat di tempat lain.
- **Satu gambar utuh setinggi penuh.** Gambar bagian atas tajam, menyambung dengan bagian bawah (langit, dinding atau hiasan berlanjut ke atas melewati garis y = 200). Aplikasi menampilkannya **persis seperti digambar: tanpa blur, tanpa bayangan**.
- **Hanya latar:** langit, dinding, kayu, daun, kain, motif. **Tanpa teks, logo, wajah atau makanan.**
- iPhone menampilkan dari garis sambungan ke atas sekitar 145–185 px; 15–55 px paling atas bisa terpotong.
- Jam (kiri, x 80–300, y 90–150), sinyal dan baterai (kanan, x 900–1120) dan kamera hitam (tengah, x 460–740) tampil putih/hitam di atasnya: **sisakan ruang**, buat area itu **tenang dan cukup gelap untuk teks putih**.
