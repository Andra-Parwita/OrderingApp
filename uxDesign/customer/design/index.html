Onde Onde customer app · design boards

Each board is a strip of phone screens. They need support.js beside them and must be opened over http (not file://). In each board's markup, the theme is set by the classes on the strip's root element: th <brand> <light|dark>. The screen ids and their positions are listed in ../data/screens.json.
