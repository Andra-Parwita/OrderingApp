Visual directions · round 2

Draft 1, 2026-10-07 · static mock-up, not a build source

Purpose: choose a new colour theme. Owner's words: "maybe change the theme to clay or a more traditional indonesian-like theme". Three directions follow: B · Clay, C · Sogan (soga-brown batik) and D · Nila & Kunyit (indigo batik, turmeric touch). Layout, spacing, type scale and radii are unchanged from src/theme/tokens.ts; only colour differs. Sage (current) is shown small at the top for reference.

How to compare: same four frames per direction (Customer menu and Seller orders, light and dark). Check calm, whether status pills read at a glance, whether the primary button and the Ready pill can be told apart, and the focus ring (one control per frame is shown focused). C and D each include one optional motif in the menu banner: say yes or no. Every pair passes WCAG AA in light and dark. Reply with a letter, and yes/no on the motif.

  • Accent is used only on the primary action, the selected language pill, the selected tab line and the focus ring (C uses a deep green for focus only).
  • Status pills are muted tints and always carry text. Ready is a green tint; the primary button is a solid fill in a different hue (brown, terracotta or indigo).
  • No boxed cards: 1px hairlines, spacing and slight surface tints only.

Comparison

B · Clay

  • Feel: warm, homely and appetising; terracotta and warm neutrals suggest a kitchen, clay pots and spices. Modern, not specifically Indonesian.
  • Strengths: a clear identity apart from every status colour; easy to read; lowest text ratio 5.12.
  • Risks: terracotta sits near red, so it must never mark errors or Cancelled; could be mistaken for a generic "warm food app".

C · Sogan

  • Feel: heritage, earthy and calm, like old sogan batik cloth; cream and soga brown read as "traditional kitchen".
  • Strengths: instantly familiar to Indonesians; very readable (warm dark text on ivory); the green focus ring is the only cool note, so keyboard focus is obvious; optional kawung motif adds character.
  • Risks: brown accent is the least "bright" of the three, so the primary button is quieter; brown plus cream can feel dated or heavy if overused; motif must stay in the banner.

D · Nila & Kunyit

  • Feel: crisp, cool and refined with a small warm spark; indigo batik with turmeric highlights.
  • Strengths: the most distinct primary button (indigo against the green Ready pill and amber Ordered pill); the turmeric "N left" badge is a natural fit; the cleanest and most modern look.
  • Risks: indigo is cooler and less "homely"; Confirmed (slate-blue pill) is the nearest neighbour to the accent hue, so keep Confirmed as a tint only; parang diagonal can look busy if the motif is ever made stronger.

Best fit for a Melbourne-based Indonesian home kitchen audience

C · Sogan is the best fit: it reads at once as Indonesian home cooking to family and friends, without costume, and it stays calm and readable on the sellers' long order lists. D is the pick if a cleaner, more modern look matters more than warmth; B is the safest but least Indonesian.
