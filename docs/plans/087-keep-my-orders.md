# 087 · Keep my orders: optional, privacy-first (customers)

**Status:** built (stages 1-4 done, 17 Oct); pending the end-of-phase gate and the builder's deploy (one migration 0029, no secrets). Decision: D-107. E2E: `e2e/p087-remember-me.spec.ts`.
**Owner, 17 Oct:** "think about customers having social login via Apple ID or Google ID, etc., or maybe passkey, so they can keep their data easily but still private enough"; "we don't need email link"; "the idea is that My orders can be easily found or retained"; "and all are optional for user".

## Today
My orders, the first name, the delivery address and per-kitchen customer codes live only in the browser (`src/api/device/*`). A new phone or cleared browser loses them; one order can be found again by its WhatsApp link or code + first name (D-075).

## Owner rulings
- **Passkey + first name** (owner "B", 17 Oct): stage 1 keeps the order list and the first name.
- **Saved delivery address, 30 days** (owner, 17 Oct: "ideally the stored address that is kept could also be matched for 30 days … if older or no order in the past 30 days, the address is deleted"): the customer's saved address may be kept with their passkey, deleted when they have had no order for 30 days. **Protection: sealed with the customer's passkey** so the server cannot read it (owner "A", 17 Oct). Flow: the customer's phone unlocks it with the passkey, checkout (Delivery) is pre-filled, and on ordering the address travels inside the order exactly as today (encrypted with `ADDRESS_KEY`, team-only, deleted a day after the menu). Phones without the passkey PRF feature: keep the address on that phone only. today's rules for comparison: order addresses on the server are deleted a day after the menu (D-099), the customer's own saved address stays on their phone with no expiry, the seller's address book stays on the seller's device (D-059).
- **Stable per-kitchen customer code + confirmed mark** (owner "A", 17 Oct: "generate some unique id and then the seller actually knows this customer id … and the seller can be more sure about the confirmation"): with the passkey, the plan 071 per-kitchen customer code is tied to the passkey account, so it is the same on every phone (e.g. C-7K3M); orders placed while unlocked carry "✓ Passkey-confirmed · C-7K3M" for the seller. The code differs per kitchen (kitchens can't link a customer across kitchens); no name, phone or email behind it.
- **Lost passkey / new phone not synced:** a 6-digit "add this phone" code from a phone that is still signed in, like sellers' devices (owner "A", 17 Oct); with no other phone, orders still open from the WhatsApp link or "Find my order".
- **Where it is offered** (owner "A" + addition, 17 Oct: "offer it as per A, for the first time in the main menu as a small non-intrusive button or something; when clicked we show information first"): Settings card; empty My orders ("Had orders on another phone? Restore them"); one small line after the 2nd order; and **the first time on the customer menu home, a small, quiet button/chip** (e.g. "🔒 Keep my orders") — tapping it opens the explanation (advantages + safety) first; only then "Turn on". Dismissable for good.
- **Name** (owner "A", 17 Oct): chip **"🔒 Remember me"** / **"🔒 Ingat aku"**; card title **"Remember me on all my phones (optional)"** / **"Ingat aku di semua HP-ku (opsional)"**. Never "account"; not "kitchen" (it covers all kitchens). Replaces "Keep my orders" in the UI.
- **Turning it off** (owner, 17 Oct: "what happens if they decide not to remember me? can they delete the passkey?"): Settings → Remember me → "Delete my saved data" (asks first) deletes everything on our side at once: the account id, the passkey's public key, the order list, the sealed address and the customer-code link. The phone keeps its own My orders, and orders still work by link. The passkey itself lives in the phone's password manager: the app tells the phone it's no longer valid (WebAuthn Signal API, `signalUnknownCredential`, where supported) and shows how to remove it by hand otherwise (iPhone: Settings → Passwords; Android: Google Password Manager). A leftover passkey is useless once our side is deleted.
- **Explained everywhere** (owner, 17 Oct: "make sure it's also in seller Help and in the customer app privacy note … instead of expansion, open a modal which can also explain this"): (1) a seller Help topic "Remember me (customers)" / "Ingat aku (pelanggan)": what it is, the ✓ Passkey-confirmed mark and code, what sellers can't see, how customers delete it; (2) the customer app's privacy note (Settings → About / privacy) changes from expanding text to a **modal** ("How we keep your data private") that covers today's rules and Remember me: what's stored, sealed address, 30-day deletion, turning it off and removing the passkey.
- **Explain what a passkey is** (owner, 17 Oct: "in the turn-on card, a bit of explanation of passkey; many people may not know what a passkey is"): a short "What's a passkey?" line in the card, with an optional "Learn more" in the privacy modal. Draft EN: "**What's a passkey?** A safer way to sign in, built into your phone. Instead of a password, you unlock with your face, fingerprint or phone PIN, the same way you unlock your phone. It's stored safely on your phone (and your phone's backup), and it only works for this app." ID: "**Apa itu passkey?** Cara masuk yang lebih aman, sudah ada di HP-mu. - **Retention:** a Remember me unused for **12 months** is deleted automatically (owner "A", 17 Oct); the sealed address separately after 30 days with no order.
- **Second kitchen app** (owner, 17 Oct: "how is the customer experience when adding a passkey in Onde Onde and then installing Dellavee, or the other way around?"): one passkey serves every kitchen (same site). ## Recommendation (Fable): D first, then A; B not now
1. **Move my orders to another phone** (QR / short code between two phones; no cloud data).
2. **Optional passkey "vault"**: the device encrypts My orders (+ name, saved address, customer codes) and the server stores only the ciphertext (≤ 8 KB) + an opaque id + passkey public key; idle 12 months → deleted; "Delete my saved data" removes everything. The server never gets a customer → orders list, so kitchens can't link a person across kitchens.
3. Apple/Google only later if Square needs a receipt email, as a second way in, never the only one.
- Everything optional: ordering, links and My orders on the same phone work as today; gentle offers only (Settings card; one quiet line after an order; "Had orders on another phone? Restore them" on an empty My orders).
- Privacy text changes: About "No sign-ups. Ever." → "No sign-ups needed … optional passkey, no email, no password"; Settings "No accounts …"; brief and overview lines; a decision amending D-007/D-090 for customers.

## Build design (design check, opus, 17 Oct)
- **Migration 0029_remember_me.sql** (add-only; 086 takes 0028): `customer_accounts` (id, first_name, code_salt, created/last used/last order), `customer_passkeys` (credential, public key, counter, prf flag), `customer_sessions` (90-day sliding, cookie `__Host-customer`), `customer_challenges`, `customer_phone_codes` (6 digits, 5 min, single use), `customer_order_links` (account, token, seller), `customer_kitchen_codes` (adopted plan 071 codes only), `customer_sealed_addresses` (v, iv, ct ≤ 2 KB per credential); `orders.customer_confirmed`.
- **Per-kitchen code** = HMAC(code_salt, "customer-code:v1:" + seller) → fits plan 071's format; shown to sellers as "C-" + 4 characters. Existing device codes are adopted only with proof (an order with that token, seller and code). An order is "✓ Passkey-confirmed" only when the request carries a valid customer session; a forged body flag is ignored; a bad cookie never blocks ordering.
- **Sealed address:** WebAuthn PRF → HKDF → AES-GCM key kept on the phone (IndexedDB); the server stores ciphertext only; the PRF output is stripped before any POST (tested). No PRF → address stays on the phone. `PUT address` refused without a confirmed order in 30 days; the hourly sweep deletes old blobs. Accounts unused 12 months are deleted (weekly).
- **Separation:** customer tables, cookie and routes (`/api/customer/*`) are separate from sellers'; a customer cookie gets 401 on seller/admin/live routes (tested); a customer passkey offered at seller sign-in (and vice versa) gets a friendly refusal; labels "<first name> · Remember me".
- **6-digit add-phone code:** 5 wrong tries per device → 15-min lock; >50 wrong tries in 5 min cancels all live codes.
- **Delete my data:** one cascade delete; the phone forgets its key and calls `signalUnknownCredential` where available; local My orders stay.
- **Seller side:** short pill "✓ C-7K3M" on order rows, "✓ Passkey-confirmed · C-7K3M" in the detail; sellers never get the account id.
- **Stages:** 1 worker auth + data (+ separation tests) · 2 orders, seller display, retention ‖ 3 client (ceremonies, PRF crypto, merge, chip, Settings card, empty My orders, after-order line, privacy modal, Help topic) · 4 e2e (virtual authenticator with/without PRF, second context restore, 6-digit join, delete) + docs + decision.
- **Deploy:** one migration, no secrets, existing crons.
