# 126 · Delivery on another day (customer picks from 1–3 days the seller offers)

**Target: 1.0.46.** **Status:** built and checked 8 Nov (directed e2e, 2 emulator rounds on tablet + phone), live 8 Nov (Worker `050816b7`); mock-up approved (owner 8 Nov: "with label should be good enough"). First use: DellaVee event **Sun 15 Nov**.

## Why (owner, 8 Nov)
"Ability for a delivery to be delivered on a different day … we don't have to worry about kitchen or cook, on when they will do the cook, just like a normal app. It's just ability for a customer to set a different delivery day, and ability for the seller to then set 'can be different day', which say give 1–3 different day options."

## Understanding (to confirm)
- Said: the menu keeps its cooking day; kitchen and cooks unchanged. Per menu the seller can switch on "Can be delivered on a different day" and offer 1–3 other days; the customer picks the delivery day when ordering.
- Assumed (questions): cut-off unchanged; the other days come after the cooking day; Pickup & delivery and labels show the day.

## Today (code map, 8 Nov)
- **No delivery date anywhere.** Delivery = `menus.delivery_available` + `delivery_note_en/_id` (0003); `shared/domain.ts:298` `Delivery = { available, note }`. Pickup windows are on the cooking day (`pickup_places`, `menu_pickup_places`). The only order-level date is for private orders (`event_date`, `event_time`, 0027).
- **Assumes delivery = cooking day:** customer order page (`customer-orders/orderInfo.ts:45-61`, `OrderPageView.tsx:632`); checkout delivery branch shows no date (`customer-menu/CheckoutViews.tsx:460-465`); share text (`components/shareText/shareText.ts:120-146`); labels (`seller-labels/LabelsScreen.tsx:196-201`); Pickup & delivery / delivery run (`seller-saturday/HandOverScreen.tsx`, `seller-orders/DeliveriesScreen.tsx`); pack and cook models (`seller-cook/packModel.ts`, `cookModel.ts`); change-fulfilment sheet (`components/change-fulfilment/ChangeFulfilment.tsx:294-325`).
- **Server timing that would break a later delivery:** auto-finish at midnight after the cooking day marks open deliveries delivered (`worker/db/menuFinish.ts:220,231`, `shared/dates.ts:168` `menuFinishesAt`); delivery addresses are wiped 24 h after the menu closes (`worker/db/address.ts:65-88`), so a delivery 2–3 days later could lose its address. Order validation: `worker/db/sellerContext.ts:483-517`.
- Docs to update: `docs/architecture/data-model.md` (:41-42, :172-179, :220), `docs/product/flows.md` (:13, :59, :65, :84).

## Owner rulings
- **No new rules or checks (owner, 8 Nov):** "we don't have to worry about cut-off order and when it can be delivered … we won't need to do logic or check … we leave it to the seller … all cut-off, order, event are as is today, it just seller has ability to have different day of delivery … in reality, seller can cook at anytime for this special delivery." → Cut-off, ordering, private orders (events), cooking day, cook and pack screens stay exactly as today; the app only records and shows the delivery day the customer picked.
- **Q1: delivery only** (owner, 8 Nov: "yep, this is for delivery only"). Pickup stays on the cooking day at the pickup places.
- **Later, pickup too (owner, 8 Nov):** "which later of course can be extended to pickup/delivery, since it's just need label and day option, the rest should be as is." → build the day choice so it can later apply to pickup with only a label and the day options (e.g. one order-level `handover_date`, not a delivery-only name); no pickup work now.
- **Q2 A (owner, 8 Nov):** with extra delivery days the automatic finish waits until **midnight after the last delivery day offered** (open deliveries → Delivered then; addresses wiped 24 h after, as today); the seller can still tap **Finish** to end early ("and also seller can also decide to tap Finish to finish early if needed"). Without extra days nothing changes.
- **Q3 (owner, 8 Nov): which days.** "Up to them, we just give them up to 3 options … just give warning if they are more than 1 day out after the event or more than 1 day early, so even Sunday, they can set Friday, Sat, Mon and the event day (as default) … but we leave it to seller itself." → The cooking day is always a delivery day (the default); the seller may add **up to 3 other days, before or after** the cooking day. **No block, only a warning** when a day is more than 1 day before or after the cooking day. Example: cooking Sun → Fri, Sat, Mon + Sun. Auto-finish follows the **latest** day (Q2).
- **Test focus (owner, 8 Nov):** DellaVee's seller uses a **Samsung Android tablet and an Android phone** → the emulator check covers the tablet (Tablet_11) and the phone (Pixel_9).
- **Q4 A (owner, 8 Nov): checkout.** When Delivery is chosen and the menu has extra days, a **Delivery day** row of day chips appears under the address, **cooking day pre-selected**; tapping another day changes it. - **No extra days = today's app (owner, 8 Nov):** "we don't need to show them if no extra delivery days is added, so it will behave like existing behaviour with only event day delivery." → With no extra days the checkout, order page, My orders, labels, share text and seller screens look and work exactly as today (no Delivery day row, no day labels).
- **Q5 A (owner, 8 Nov): seller sets the days** in the menu **Details** step (tablet), under the Delivery switch, as **day chips, no calendar**: "maybe just give it 2 days before and after instead of calendar days" → a strip of **2 days before, the cooking day (fixed, always on) and 2 days after**; tap up to 3 of the 4 other days; the soft warning shows on the days 2 away. ## Build (stage 1)
- Migration **0035**: `menus.extra_delivery_days` TEXT NULL (JSON array of up to 3 ISO dates) and `orders.handover_date` TEXT NULL (the chosen day when it is not the cooking day; NULL = cooking day; named for later pickup use). Add-only, applied before the Worker.
- Server: menu Details save/validate (max 3, distinct, within 2 days of the cooking day, never the cooking day); order create accepts `handoverDate` only for delivery and only if it is one of the menu's days (else `invalid_request`); public menu, seller orders, customer order, backup/restore carry the fields; auto-finish uses the latest offered day (`menuFinishesAt`).
- Client: Details chips (2 before/after, warning for days 2 away); checkout Delivery day chips (cooking day pre-selected); Order placed / order page / My orders / WhatsApp order text show the chosen day; seller order row + detail + label + printed order show a day tag only for an extra day; Pickup & delivery → Delivery and the delivery run sorted by day; share post line; phone read-only menu lists the days. No extra days = today's app.
